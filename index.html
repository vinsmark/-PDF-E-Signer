How it works
1

Select a PDF

Use the Document panel to upload your file. The preview appears on the left.

2

Add your signature

Upload a PNG or JPG image of your handwritten or digital signature.

3

Position & apply

Drag the signature onto the document and resize with the corner handle. Click Apply signature.
